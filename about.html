Studio Origin

The Science of Frictionless Human Velocity

VELOMETRIC™ was founded in 2021 by orthopedic physical therapists and Formula 1 aerodynamic engineers who recognized that cycling fitting was trapped in the 1990s—relying on static plumb lines and eye measurements.

We built an uncompromising medical-grade laboratory where every millimeter is validated through high-speed synchronized infrared motion-capture, wireless tactile pressure arrays, and continuous VO2 metabolic gas telemetry.
